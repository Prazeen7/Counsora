# Counsora – Study Abroad Counseling Platform

Counsora is a web-based study abroad counseling platform designed to help education counselors provide structured, accurate, and destination-specific guidance to students during counseling sessions.

The platform brings together essential information about study destinations, academic programs, educational institutions, documentation requirements, cost estimations, and study guides in one centralized system.

**Live Application:** https://counsora.vercel.app/

## Key Features

### 1. Destination-Specific Information
Select a preferred study destination and explore relevant educational opportunities, requirements, and guidance specific to that country.

### 2. Academic Program Exploration
Browse available academic programs and courses to help students identify suitable study options based on their educational background, interests, and career objectives.

### 3. Institution Exploration
Explore universities and educational institutions across different study destinations, allowing counselors to guide students in selecting appropriate institutions.

### 4. Documentation Requirements
Access information about the documents required for studying abroad, helping counselors explain application requirements and preparation processes to students.

### 5. Cost Estimation
Review estimated study-related expenses, including tuition fees and other associated costs, to support financial planning and informed decision-making.

### 6. Study Guide
Access structured study-abroad guidance to assist counselors in explaining essential processes, requirements, and considerations throughout the student's educational journey.

## Purpose

Counsora is developed specifically for education counselors and study-abroad consultancies to streamline the student counseling process.

By centralizing destination-specific educational information, the platform reduces the need to navigate multiple resources during counseling sessions and enables counselors to deliver more organized, consistent, and informative guidance.
